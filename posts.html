Know it is working, with just 3 numbers

You do not need to track a dozen metrics. That is how people quit. Every option uses the same simple check, once a month, in about 5 minutes.

  • Did we post? Posts published this month. The target is 8: 2 a week, as planned.
  • Did people see it? Reach per post: total reach divided by the number of posts. This shows whether each post is reaching more people, not just whether you posted more often.
  • Did anyone act? Total actions: messages started, link clicks, and calls (calls only if your Page has a Call button), added together.

No promises about follower counts or sales. Just clear proof of consistency, visibility, and action.

Do not leave comments hanging

Posting is only half the job. When someone comments and hears nothing back, the conversation, and often the sale, stops there. Replying also keeps the conversation going, which can help a post be shown to more people. Every option includes help with replies.

How it works, in every option: Muse drafts a reply, a person approves the exact wording, and only then is it posted. Nothing replies automatically on its own. That approval step is what keeps replies safe on price questions, complaints, and anything sensitive.

  • Thank you or compliment: a short, warm reply, by name when possible.
  • Question: hours, price, availability, or which option fits them best, answered plainly.
  • Ready to book or buy: one clear next step, not a sales pitch.
  • Problem or complaint: never argued with in public. A calm draft is prepared and flagged for the owner, and the conversation moves to a message or a phone call.

Questions, answered plainly

Do I need to pay for Facebook ads?

No. Every option is about organic posting: showing up consistently in front of people who already follow you and the people they share you with. Ads are a separate choice, and nothing here requires them.

Do I need a Facebook Business Page?

Yes, a business Page, not just a personal profile. The Playbook is written for business Pages. If you only have a personal profile, the Managed Sprint includes setup help.

Where do the photos come from?

From you, on your phone. Every Custom Posts Pack and Managed Sprint post comes with a specific photo idea, and the Playbook includes a checklist. Real photos of your shop, work, and team fit local businesses better than stock photos.

What if a post is wrong or I do not like it?

Edit it. The posts are yours. The Custom Posts Pack includes 7 days of email support for factual fixes after delivery. You approve every post before it goes live in every option, so nothing posts automatically without you.

How fast do I get the Custom Posts Pack?

3–5 business days after your intake form is complete. If your form is missing key details, that clock starts once those details arrive.

Can I get a call with the Custom Posts Pack?

A call is not included, on purpose. That is how the pack stays at $149. You get a recorded walkthrough instead. If you want a live walkthrough, add the 30-minute call for $75 at checkout.

Will this guarantee more customers?

No honest service can guarantee that. What this does deliver is the part most businesses struggle with: showing up consistently, with accurate, well-written posts, week after week. That consistency gives Facebook, and your customers, something to respond to.

Do comment replies post automatically?

No. Muse drafts the reply and a person approves the exact wording before anything is posted, in every option. Problems and complaints are flagged for the owner instead of being answered in public.
